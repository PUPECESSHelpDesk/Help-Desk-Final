Common questions,
clear signals.

Small questions are still worth answering well. Start with the topics students ask us about most.

01Where can I find the ECE lab schedule?
The latest lab schedule is posted in the ECE student portal under Resources → Laboratory. If your section is missing or conflicts with another class, submit a ticket and include your block and preferred schedule.
02How do I request a component kit?
Submit a component request through the department inventory form at least two working days before your lab. Include your course code, experiment number, and the quantities you need.
03My simulation is not matching the lecture output.
Attach a screenshot of your schematic, simulator version, and expected output to a ticket. The teaching team can usually spot a grounding, model, or initial-condition issue quickly.
04Where do I ask about enrollment concerns?
For prerequisites, section changes, and enrollment holds, choose Enrollment & records in the ticket form. These requests are routed to the program coordinator.
